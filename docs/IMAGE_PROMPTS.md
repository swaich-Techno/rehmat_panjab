# Image prompts

Use these to commission stills. **No type in the frame.** Leave room for editorial type to sit beside the picture. Palette stays in ink, forest, sage, cream, sand, amber, rose-metal, wine. No purple-blue cinema lighting. No stock perfume-bottle clichés, no Golden Temple, no Punjab map, no tractor, no wheat field.

## A — Musk Rizali hero, 4:5

A single glass attar bottle, matte forest cap, a third-full of translucent amber oil, standing on warm ivory paper. Morning side-light from the left, long quiet shadow to the right. Large negative space in the upper third. Shallow depth. No label text, no logo, no droplets flying.

**Why:** PDP left column and homepage scene 04. 4:5 holds a bottle without looking like a marketplace tile. Negative space lets the number `01` sit in type.

## B — Oil meniscus, 16:9

Macro of perfume oil against glass: a thick meniscus, honeyed, slow, not watery. Colour from amber into rose-metal. Soft gradient, no bubbles that read as soda, no splash.

**Why:** Homepage liquid scene and oil-veil replacement. Wide frame so the oil can run edge to edge under type.

## C — Wrist, 3:2

A wrist and the first two knuckles, warm skin, a thin sheen of oil catching morning light. No jewellery logo, no watch face type, no gender posing. Cream linen at the edge of frame.

**Why:** Close-to-skin story. 3:2 leaves a band for the line “Perfume oil, close to skin.”

## D — Collection lineup, 16:9

Five bottles in a staggered, uneven row — not centered, not equally spaced — on a mist-green ground. One bottle closer. Soft daylight. Caps mix forest and metal. No names on glass.

**Why:** Collection opener. Wide frame, irregular rhythm so it does not become a 3-card grid.

## E — Origin table, 4:5

A table of materials only: crumbled dark soil, a blade of grass, a torn sheet of ivory paper, a shard of clear glass, a dull metal disc, a folded textile. Morning raking light. No monuments, maps, or harvest clichés.

**Why:** Homepage scene 08. Portrait crop so the stack of materials can be tall and tactile.

## F — Note still life, 1:1

Loose saffron threads, a dry rose petal, a chip of resin, a curl of wood. Sand-coloured cloth. Plenty of empty cream around the cluster.

**Why:** Note passages. Square crop for overlapping layers beside large serif words (MUSK, OUD, AMBER).

## G — Garden atmosphere, 16:9

Out-of-focus leaf and sage colour, a suggestion of peach light in the corner, almost abstract. No identifiable landmark. No people.

**Why:** Finder and next-drop atmospheres. Wide, empty, so typographic answers can sit on top.

## H — Paper archive, 5:4

A stack of unlabelled paper, a metal clip, a glass rod. Evening charcoal at the left, amber falling across the paper. No writing visible.

**Why:** Private-house / vault still. Almost square so the vault aperture can overlay.

## I — Empty courtyard morning, 16:9

A wall the colour of sand, a strip of shadow, a wet stone, soft leaf-green at the far edge. No temple, no script on the wall, no decorative arch used as a tourist signal.

**Why:** Origin and brand films. Landscape with a large quiet field for the wordmark.

## J — Unopened next oil, 4:5

A bottle silhouette behind frosted glass or thin cloth, colour only suggested (warm ivory to muted burgundy). Not a teaser with a fake name. No “coming soon” type in the photograph.

**Why:** Next Rehmat page. Portrait, mystery without clickbait badges.

## Replacement rule

Keep current SVG files in `public/images/placeholders/` until a commissioned file is in place. Catalogue entries use `placeholder: true` until then.
